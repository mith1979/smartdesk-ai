# NovaTech Solutions — Payday and Payslip Queries

This is a fictional payroll guide for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All schedules and processes are demonstration rules.

## Salary Payment Schedule
Full-time employees are paid monthly by bank transfer.

Payday is the last working day of each calendar month.
For this policy, working days are Monday through Friday,
excluding company holidays.

If the last calendar day is a weekend or company holiday,
payday moves to the preceding working day.

This document does not contain the company holiday calendar.

## Payslip Availability
Payslips are available in the fictional NovaTech People Portal
by the end of payday.

To find your payslip:
1. Sign in to the People Portal.
2. Select Payroll > Payslips.
3. Choose the relevant month.
4. View or download your payslip.

If you cannot sign in, follow the Password Reset Guide
or contact IT.

## Missing Salary Payment
If payment is not visible by the end of payday:
1. Check the bank account registered with Payroll.
2. Contact payroll@novatech.example on the next working day.
3. Include your employee email and the affected pay month.

Payroll acknowledges queries within 2 working days.
This is an acknowledgment target, not a resolution guarantee.

Do not send full bank account details or bank statements
through SmartDesk.

## Incorrect Payslip or Salary Amount
Contact Payroll with:
- Employee email.
- Affected pay month.
- The payslip line item you believe is incorrect.
- A brief explanation of the discrepancy.

Submit sensitive supporting documents only through the
secure channel specified by Payroll.

SmartDesk cannot access your personal salary records
or calculate an individual correction from this guide.

## Updating Bank Details
Open People Portal > Payroll > Bank Details
and use the secure update form.

Submit changes by the 15th calendar day of the month
for consideration in that month's payroll.

Payroll must verify and confirm the change.
Late or unverified changes may take effect in a later cycle.

Never provide bank credentials, passwords, or verification codes.

## Reimbursements
Approved expense claims are paid separately from salary.
Follow the Reimbursement Policy for its payment timeline.

## Support and Limits
Payroll questions: payroll@novatech.example.
Portal access problems: helpdesk@novatech.example.

These are demonstration addresses.

This guide does not define tax calculations, bonus eligibility,
stock vesting, final settlement, or individual salary amounts.
Refer those questions to Payroll.
