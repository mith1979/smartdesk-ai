# NovaTech Solutions — Workplace Code of Conduct

This is a fictional guide generated for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All company rules, contacts, and processes are demonstration values.

## Scope and Responsibilities
Employees are expected to behave respectfully, communicate honestly, protect company information, and use company resources for authorized purposes. These expectations apply to office work, remote work, company meetings, and work-related communication channels.

## Respectful Collaboration
Discuss work disagreements constructively and avoid threats, personal insults, harassment, or discriminatory behavior. Raise persistent workplace concerns with HR at hr@novatech.example. Reporting a concern does not require the employee to determine whether a policy violation has occurred.

## Conflicts of Interest
Disclose a potential personal or financial conflict affecting a work decision to compliance@novatech.example before participating in that decision. Provide a brief description and request guidance. Disclosure does not itself approve the activity. This guide sets no gift-value threshold and does not replace a full anti-bribery policy.

## Information and Company Resources
Follow the Confidential Information guide when handling sensitive records. Do not use another employee account or share passwords. Request application access and software through the existing IT approval processes. A business need does not remove the requirement for approval.

## Reporting and Limits
Send general employment concerns to HR and ethical or potential policy-violation concerns to Compliance. The Reporting Concerns policy protects good-faith reports against retaliation. SmartDesk must not promise anonymity, an investigation outcome, or a disciplinary result. This guide does not define investigation or response deadlines.
