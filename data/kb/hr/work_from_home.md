# NovaTech Solutions — Work-from-Home Policy

This is a fictional policy for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All rules below are demonstration rules.

## Eligibility
Full-time employees, including employees on probation, may request
work from home if their duties can be performed remotely.

Roles requiring physical presence need a separate arrangement
approved by the reporting manager and HR.

## Weekly Schedule
Eligible employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week,
subject to manager approval.

The remaining scheduled working days are office days.
Unused remote days do not carry forward to another week.

## Request and Approval
1. Open the fictional NovaTech People Portal.
2. Select Work Arrangement > Remote Work Request.
3. Enter the requested dates and a brief reason.
4. Submit at least 2 working days before the first requested date.
5. Wait for your reporting manager's approval.

A recurring weekly schedule can be approved for up to 3 months.
Submit a new request when that approval expires.

## Urgent Requests and Exceptions
For an unexpected situation, contact your manager as soon as practical.
Record the request in the People Portal on the same working day.

More than 2 remote days in a week requires approval from both
the reporting manager and HR. Approval is not automatic.

## Availability
Follow your agreed working hours and attend scheduled meetings.
Inform your manager if connectivity prevents you from working.

Working from home is a working day and does not deduct leave.
If you are too unwell to work, follow the Leave Policy instead.

## Equipment and Security
Use a company-managed laptop.
Use the company VPN when accessing resources that require it.
Follow the VPN Setup Guide for access and connection instructions.

Do not share employee accounts or company devices with others.
Do not transfer company information to personal email accounts.

## Expenses
This policy does not automatically reimburse home internet,
furniture, or other remote-work expenses.
Refer expense questions to the Reimbursement Policy or HR.

## Support and Limits
Contact hr@novatech.example for policy questions.
Contact helpdesk@novatech.example for technical problems.

These are demonstration addresses.

This document does not authorize working from another country
or define a permanent fully remote arrangement.
Refer those requests to HR.
