# NovaTech Solutions — Probation Review

This is a fictional guide generated for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All company rules, contacts, and processes are demonstration values.

## Standard Period
The demonstration standard probation period is 90 calendar days from the employment start date. HR must confirm any employee-specific period stated in an employment document. SmartDesk has no access to an employee contract and must not infer an individual confirmation date from this general guide alone.

## Setting Expectations
During the first working week, the reporting manager discusses role responsibilities, initial objectives, and support needs. The employee should ask for clarification when expectations are unclear. Probation does not remove the full-time employee eligibility described in the Leave, Work-from-Home, and Reimbursement policies; their normal approval rules still apply.

## Review Meeting
The manager arranges a review before the standard probation period ends. The discussion considers agreed objectives, feedback, and any additional support needed. Employees should contact their manager if no review has been scheduled near the end of the period.

## Written Outcome
HR communicates the outcome in writing after the review and required approvals. Reaching day 90 does not automatically confirm employment. Any extension or different arrangement must be confirmed by HR in writing. Employees should contact hr@novatech.example for questions about their own status.

## Support and Limits
This guide does not define a maximum extension, termination procedure, notice entitlement, or a guaranteed confirmation outcome. It does not replace an employment document or provide legal advice. Do not put contracts or sensitive employee records into SmartDesk.
