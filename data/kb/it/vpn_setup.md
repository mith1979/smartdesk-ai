# NovaTech Solutions — VPN Setup and Troubleshooting

This is a fictional IT guide for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
The client name, portal, and server are demonstration values.

## Eligibility
VPN access is available to full-time employees using
company-managed Windows, macOS, or Ubuntu laptops.

Personal devices are not supported.
Your manager must approve access, and IT must enable it
before your first connection.

## Requesting Access
Submit a VPN access request to helpdesk@novatech.example.
Include your employee email, manager's name, and business reason.

IT confirms when access is enabled.
This policy does not guarantee an activation time.

## Installing the Client
1. Open the fictional NovaTech Software Center on your laptop.
2. Find "NovaTech VPN".
3. Select Install.
4. If the client is missing or installation fails, contact IT.

Use only the package provided through the Software Center.
Do not download a client from an unofficial website.

## Connecting
1. Confirm that your normal internet connection works.
2. Open NovaTech VPN.
3. Enter vpn.novatech.example as the server.
4. Sign in using your NovaTech employee email and password.
5. Enter the code from your registered authenticator.
6. Confirm that the client displays "Connected".

Disconnect from the VPN when you finish accessing internal resources.

## Invalid Credentials
Confirm that you are using your employee email and current password.
If you recently reset your password, update any saved VPN credentials.

Avoid repeatedly retrying an old password.
See the Password Reset Guide if you cannot sign in.

## MFA Failure
Check automatic date and time on your phone and try a fresh code.
If your authenticator is unavailable, follow the MFA Recovery Guide.

SmartDesk cannot bypass MFA.

## Connection Timeout
1. Disconnect the VPN.
2. Check whether ordinary websites open without the VPN.
3. If internet access works, close and reopen the VPN client.
4. Retry the connection once.
5. If it still times out, contact IT.

Do not disable your firewall or endpoint protection.

## Certificate Warning
Stop the connection and contact IT.
Do not ignore certificate warnings or disable certificate validation.

## Connected but Internal Resource Unavailable
Check whether other authorized internal resources are accessible.
Record the affected resource and exact error message.

VPN connectivity does not automatically grant application permissions.
Contact IT if the problem persists.

## Information for an IT Ticket
Include:
- Employee email.
- Operating system and VPN client version.
- Exact error message with secrets removed.
- Approximate failure time.
- Whether normal internet access works.
- Whether the VPN displays "Connected".

Never include passwords, MFA codes, or recovery codes.

## Limits
This guide does not define advanced VPN settings,
personal-device access, or server outage recovery procedures.

Contact: helpdesk@novatech.example.
