# NovaTech Solutions — Reimbursement Policy

This is a fictional policy for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All amounts and timelines are demonstration rules.
Amounts are in US dollars (USD).

## Eligibility
Full-time employees, including employees on probation, may
claim approved business expenses.

An expense must have a business purpose and meet the
approval and documentation requirements below.

## Eligible Expenses
- Manager-approved business travel and accommodation.
- Manager-approved purchases of business supplies.
- Home internet expenses under an approved remote-work arrangement:
  up to $40 per calendar month, limited to the actual amount paid.

Unused home internet allowance does not carry forward.

Personal meals, commuting, home furniture, and personal devices
are not reimbursable under this policy.

Software purchases must follow the Software Request Guide.
Employees must not buy software independently and assume
they will be reimbursed.

## Approval Before Spending
Obtain written manager approval before incurring an expense.

For home internet, an approved remote-work arrangement
and manager approval for the reimbursement are both required.

Expenses without prior approval are not automatically eligible.
HR and the manager must review any exception.

## Submitting a Claim
1. Open the fictional NovaTech People Portal.
2. Select Expenses > New Claim.
3. Enter the expense date, category, amount, and business purpose.
4. Attach an itemized receipt and evidence of prior approval.
5. Submit within 30 calendar days of the expense date.

For home internet, submit one claim per service month and
identify that month in the description.

Do not submit the same expense more than once.

## Review and Payment
The manager reviews the business purpose and approval.
Finance checks the receipt, amount, and policy compliance.

Approved claims are paid within 10 working days after
final Finance approval, separately from salary.

Time spent waiting for missing information is not included
in the payment timeline.

## Missing Receipt or Late Submission
A missing receipt or submission after 30 calendar days
requires an exception review by the manager and Finance.
Approval is not guaranteed.

Contact hr@novatech.example to request guidance.

## Rejected or Incorrect Claims
The rejection notice explains the reason.
Correct the existing claim and resubmit if permitted.

If you believe the decision is incorrect, contact HR with
the claim ID and a brief explanation.

## Support and Limits
Contact hr@novatech.example for policy and submission questions.
This is a demonstration address.

This policy does not define international travel allowances,
foreign-currency conversion, or tax treatment.
Refer those questions to HR.
