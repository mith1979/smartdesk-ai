# NovaTech Solutions — HR and Compliance Contacts

This fictional company guide is adapted from selected
HR dataset records for the SmartDesk AI capstone.

## When to Contact HR
Contact HR for questions about:
- The employee handbook.
- Employment policies.
- Employee benefits.
- General workplace matters.

Email: hr@novatech.example.

## When to Contact Compliance
Contact Compliance for questions about:
- Legal or regulatory compliance.
- Ethical concerns.
- Potential violations of company policy.
- The Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Policy.

Email: compliance@novatech.example.

## Compliance Team Responsibilities
The Compliance Team oversees implementation and enforcement
of the Anti-Bribery and Anti-Corruption Policy.

The team provides guidance, answers policy questions,
and helps employees understand their responsibilities.

## Support and Limits
The email addresses above are demonstration values.

This guide identifies the appropriate contact.
It does not define gift-value limits, investigation procedures,
response deadlines, or the full Anti-Bribery Policy.

Refer questions about those details to Compliance.
