# NovaTech Solutions — Office Printer Setup

This is a fictional guide generated for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All company rules, contacts, and processes are demonstration values.

## Scope
This guide covers adding an office printer published in the NovaTech Software Center on a company-managed laptop. It does not cover personal printers, printer hardware repairs, or changes to network infrastructure. Printing sensitive records remains subject to the applicable information-handling rules.

## Adding an Office Printer
Connect to the office employee network. Open NovaTech Software Center, select the printer matching your office and floor, and install the published printer package. Print a non-sensitive test page. If the printer is missing from the catalog, contact helpdesk@novatech.example rather than downloading a driver from an unofficial source.

## Wrong Printer or Failed Job
Check that the selected printer matches the location you intended. Check the application for a paused job or an error. Cancel only your own unwanted duplicate jobs. If the error persists, report the printer name, office location, laptop operating system, error text, and when the problem started. Do not include the document being printed in SmartDesk.

## Repeated Disconnections
Record whether the issue affects one device or several colleagues and whether the printer previously worked. Contact IT with these observations. Repeated disconnections require IT review; employees must not change printer IP addresses, install unapproved drivers, or alter network settings.

## Support and Limits
IT decides whether access, configuration, or hardware investigation is required. This guide does not establish a repair procedure, replacement entitlement, or guaranteed fix. Do not promise that reinstalling a package will resolve every printer problem.
