# NovaTech Solutions — Policy Reviews and Updates

This is a fictional company policy adapted from selected
HR dataset records for the SmartDesk AI capstone.

## Review Schedule
NovaTech Solutions reviews company policies annually.

Policies may also be updated between annual reviews when
necessary to maintain compliance with applicable laws
and reflect best practices.

## Notification of Significant Changes
Employees are notified of significant policy changes through
official company email or internal announcements.

## Corrections to Approved Documents
If an error is discovered in an approved policy document:
1. A revised version is drafted.
2. The correction is clearly documented.
3. The revised document goes through approval again.
4. After approval, the corrected version replaces the previous
   version as the current policy.
