# NovaTech Solutions — Equipment Allocation

This is a fictional guide for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All processes and contacts are demonstration values.

## Standard Equipment
Full-time employees receive:
- One company-managed laptop.
- One laptop charger.
- One headset.

IT selects the laptop model and operating system based on
role requirements and available inventory.
A specific brand or model is not guaranteed.

## Allocation Process
HR informs IT of the employee's start date and reporting manager.
The manager confirms the role's equipment requirements.

HR includes collection or delivery instructions in the
employee's welcome email.

Equipment is intended to be available for the first working day,
but this guide does not guarantee a delivery date.

## Office Collection
Follow the collection instructions in the welcome email.
IT records the laptop's asset ID and asks the employee
to acknowledge receipt.

## Remote Delivery
Confirm your delivery details through the HR onboarding process.
Do not provide your full home address in a SmartDesk conversation.

HR and IT coordinate delivery and communicate available updates
through the onboarding email thread.

## Missing or Incorrect Equipment
Contact helpdesk@novatech.example and notify your manager.

Include:
- Employee email or registered onboarding email.
- Start date.
- Missing or incorrect item.
- Asset ID, if equipment has already been received.

Do not include passwords or identity documents.

## Additional Equipment
Additional items outside the standard allocation require
manager approval and IT review.

Approval depends on business need and availability.
Do not purchase equipment and assume reimbursement.

This guide does not establish eligibility for monitors,
home furniture, or personal devices.

## Employee Responsibilities
Keep company equipment secure.
Do not share it with family members or other unauthorized users.
Do not install unapproved software.

Follow IT instructions for maintenance and software updates.

## Support and Limits
Contact: helpdesk@novatech.example.
This is a demonstration address.

This guide does not define repair procedures, replacement
eligibility, lost-device response, or monitor troubleshooting.
Refer these issues to IT.
