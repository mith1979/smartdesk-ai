# NovaTech Solutions — Leave Policy

This is a fictional policy for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All allowances below are invented demonstration rules.

## Eligibility and Policy Year
This policy applies to full-time employees, including employees
on probation. The leave year runs from January 1 to December 31.

Employees joining during the year receive prorated allowances:
annual allowance × remaining calendar months ÷ 12.
The joining month counts as a full month.
Balances are rounded up to the nearest half-day.

## Leave Allowances
- Casual leave: 12 working days per year.
- Sick leave: 8 working days per year.
- Earned leave: 15 working days per year.
- Leave can be requested in half-day or full-day units.
- Weekends and company holidays do not deduct from leave balances.

## Requesting Planned Leave
1. Open the fictional NovaTech People Portal.
2. Select Leave > New Request.
3. Choose the leave type and dates.
4. Submit the request to your reporting manager.
5. Wait for approval before treating the leave as confirmed.

Submit casual leave at least 2 working days in advance.
Submit earned leave at least 5 working days in advance.
Managers should respond within 2 working days.
If a request remains pending, contact hr@novatech.example.

## Sick Leave
Notify your manager before your scheduled start time when possible.
If illness prevents notification, notify them as soon as practical.
Record the absence in the People Portal within 2 working days
after returning.

For more than 3 consecutive working days of sick leave,
HR requires a medical certificate. Submit it directly to HR;
do not include medical documents in a SmartDesk ticket.

## Carry-Forward
- Unused casual leave expires at year-end.
- Unused sick leave expires at year-end.
- Up to 5 unused earned-leave days carry into the next year.
- Carried earned leave must be used by March 31 or it expires.
- Carried earned leave is deducted before the new year's allowance.
- Leave cannot be encashed under this demonstration policy.

## Insufficient Balance and Cancellation
If your balance is insufficient, contact HR to discuss unpaid leave.
Unpaid leave requires approval from your manager and HR.

To cancel approved leave, submit a cancellation in the People Portal.
The balance is restored after your manager approves the cancellation.

## Support and Limits
Contact hr@novatech.example for leave-policy questions.
This is a demonstration address and does not receive email.

This document does not define parental leave, bereavement leave,
or compensatory off. SmartDesk must refer those questions to HR.
