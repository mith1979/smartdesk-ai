# NovaTech Solutions — Stock Option Questions

This is a fictional guide generated for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All company rules, contacts, and processes are demonstration values.

## Scope
This fictional overview explains how to find information about a stock option grant. Employment at NovaTech does not automatically establish eligibility for stock options. Only the employee written grant documentation and the applicable plan can establish their terms.

## Common Terms
A stock option is a right to purchase shares under specified terms; it is not the same as a cash salary payment. Vesting describes when an option becomes exercisable under the grant terms. The exercise price is the purchase price specified for the option. These definitions do not establish an employee grant value or a return on investment.

## Finding Grant Information
Review the grant notice and plan documents supplied through the company approved secure channel. If documents are missing or unclear, contact payroll@novatech.example with your employee email and a brief request for the appropriate equity-plan contact. Do not send account credentials, grant documents, tax identifiers, or investment details through SmartDesk.

## Questions Requiring Individual Review
Ask the designated plan contact about eligibility, vesting dates, exercise procedures, expiry, or the effect of leaving the company. Payroll can direct the request to that contact. Do not assume a standard vesting schedule, exercise window, or guaranteed share value.

## Support and Limits
This guide does not provide a vesting schedule, tax calculation, investment recommendation, or promise of a grant. SmartDesk cannot determine an employee vested balance, exercise an option, change a grant, or calculate a financial outcome.
