# NovaTech Solutions — Performance Review Process

This is a fictional guide generated for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All company rules, contacts, and processes are demonstration values.

## Review Cycle
The standard annual performance review cycle runs in December for full-time employees. HR communicates the opening date and submission deadlines through official company channels. A performance review is distinct from a probation review, a salary decision, or an expense approval.

## Employee Preparation
When the review task appears in NovaTech People Portal, open Performance > Annual Review. Record work objectives, completed outcomes, relevant examples, and learning needs. Submit the self-review by the deadline shown in the task. Do not place confidential client information or another employee personal records into the review.

## Manager Discussion
The reporting manager schedules a discussion after the employee self-review. Discuss achievements, challenges, feedback, and proposed objectives for the next period. Record agreed objectives in the portal. Employees should ask the manager to clarify feedback they do not understand.

## Missing Task or Disputed Record
If the review task is missing, contact hr@novatech.example with your employee email and team. If a recorded statement appears incorrect, discuss it with your manager and request HR guidance where needed. Do not submit duplicate tasks or alter another employee review.

## Support and Limits
Completing a performance review does not automatically create entitlement to a pay rise, promotion, bonus, or stock grant. This guide does not define rating formulas, compensation thresholds, individual outcomes, or appeal deadlines. SmartDesk cannot retrieve a private review or change a rating.
