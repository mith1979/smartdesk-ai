# NovaTech Solutions — Workplace Harassment Concerns

This is a fictional guide generated for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All company rules, contacts, and processes are demonstration values.

## Scope
NovaTech prohibits workplace harassment, including unwanted threatening, humiliating, or discriminatory conduct in work-related settings. This applies to in-person interactions, remote meetings, and work communication channels. Employees may raise a concern even when they are unsure how to classify an incident.

## Where to Raise a Concern
Contact hr@novatech.example for workplace harassment concerns. If the concern involves the normal HR contact, or involves an ethical or potential policy violation requiring Compliance guidance, contact compliance@novatech.example. Employees do not need manager permission to raise a good-faith concern.

## Information to Provide
Describe what happened, approximate dates, relevant work context, and whether the behavior is ongoing. Provide sensitive evidence directly through a channel agreed with HR or Compliance. Do not upload medical records, identity documents, private conversations, or unnecessary personal details into SmartDesk. A support ticket may contain a brief request for HR contact without the detailed allegation.

## Protection Against Retaliation
The Reporting Concerns policy prohibits punishment, harassment, or adverse action against people who report concerns in good faith. Report suspected retaliation to Compliance. A report does not guarantee a particular investigation finding or disciplinary action.

## Support and Limits
HR and Compliance determine the appropriate next steps. This guide does not define an acknowledgment deadline, investigation duration, anonymous reporting channel, or confidentiality guarantee. SmartDesk should offer a contact route without conducting an investigation or promising a result.
