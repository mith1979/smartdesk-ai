# NovaTech Solutions — Shared-Drive Access

This fictional guide was created for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
The source ticket inspired the topic but supplied no verified fix.

## Scope
This guide covers requesting access and reporting permission
errors for company-managed shared folders.

## Requesting Access
Contact helpdesk@novatech.example with:
- Your NovaTech employee email.
- The shared-folder name or approved internal path.
- Your business reason.
- Whether you need read-only or editing access.
- Your manager's written approval.

The folder owner approves the permission level.
IT provisions access after manager and folder-owner approval.

## Access Denied or Permission Error
1. Confirm you are using your NovaTech employee account.
2. Confirm the folder path with the folder owner.
3. If working remotely, connect to the company VPN
   when the folder requires internal network access.
4. If access was recently granted, sign out of the affected
   application and sign in again.
5. If the error continues, contact IT.

Connecting to the VPN does not automatically grant folder access.

## Previously Working Access
If access previously worked, tell IT:
- When you last accessed the folder successfully.
- When the failure started.
- Whether other approved folders remain accessible.
- Whether your role or team recently changed.

These details help investigation; they do not establish
the cause of the failure.

## Information for a Support Ticket
Include:
- Employee email.
- Folder name or internal path.
- Exact error message.
- Approximate failure time.
- Whether you are in the office or working remotely.
- Troubleshooting steps already attempted.

Do not attach confidential folder contents or credentials.
Redact sensitive information from screenshots.

## Restrictions
Do not use another employee's account.
Do not change folder permissions yourself unless authorized.
Do not copy company files to personal storage to bypass access controls.

## Support and Limits
Contact: helpdesk@novatech.example.
This is a demonstration address.

This guide does not define server administration procedures,
file recovery, or a guaranteed resolution time.
Persistent errors require IT investigation.
