# NovaTech Solutions — Understanding Salary Components

This is a fictional guide generated for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All company rules, contacts, and processes are demonstration values.

## Scope
This guide explains common salary terms used in the fictional NovaTech payroll process. An employee compensation statement and payslip provide their actual figures. SmartDesk does not have access to individual salaries, bank records, or payroll calculations.

## Gross Pay and Net Pay
Gross pay is the salary and applicable earnings before deductions. Net pay is the amount remaining after the deductions shown on the payslip. Deductions may differ between employees and pay periods. This guide defines no fixed deduction percentage or formula for predicting an employee take-home pay.

## Variable Pay and Reimbursements
An amount described as variable pay or a bonus is subject to its separately communicated terms. A performance review does not automatically guarantee payment. Approved business reimbursements are paid separately from salary under the Reimbursement Policy; they should not be assumed to arrive with the monthly salary payment.

## Checking a Difference
Compare the relevant payslip line items with your compensation statement and any written change notice. If an amount appears incorrect, email payroll@novatech.example with the pay month, line-item name, and a short description.
