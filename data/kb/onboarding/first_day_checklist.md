# NovaTech Solutions — First-Day Checklist

This is a fictional onboarding guide for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All processes and contacts are demonstration values.

## Before Your First Day
HR sends a welcome email to your registered onboarding email
address at least 2 working days before your start date.

The email includes:
- Start time and reporting instructions.
- Your reporting manager's name.
- Orientation schedule.
- Instructions for collecting or receiving your company laptop.

If the email has not arrived by the working day before your
start date, contact hr@novatech.example.

## Arrival and Orientation
Follow the arrival instructions in your welcome email.
Remote starters should use the meeting details supplied by HR.

Attend HR orientation and confirm your reporting manager
and team contacts.

## Equipment
Follow the equipment collection or delivery instructions
in your welcome email.

If your laptop has not arrived or is unavailable, notify
your manager and helpdesk@novatech.example.

Do not use a personal device for company work without
explicit authorization from IT.

## Account Setup
1. Follow the account-enrollment invitation sent to your
   registered onboarding email address.
2. Complete initial account setup.
3. Enroll your authenticator using the MFA Setup and Recovery guide.
4. Confirm that you can access company email and the People Portal.

The enrollment invitation expires after 24 hours.
Contact IT if it expires or never arrives.

Never share passwords, MFA codes, or recovery codes.

## Required Applications
Use the NovaTech Software Center for approved applications.
Follow the Software Installation and License Requests guide
if an application is missing or requires a license.

VPN access requires manager approval and IT activation.
It is not automatically enabled for every new employee.

## HR Tasks
In the People Portal:
- Review your employee profile and report errors to HR.
- Read and acknowledge assigned company policies.
- Complete the onboarding tasks assigned to you.

Use the Leave Policy and Work-from-Home Policy for the
corresponding request and approval rules.

## End-of-Day Check
Confirm with your manager that:
- Your equipment is available.
- You can access your account and required applications.
- Any blocked onboarding tasks have been reported.

## Support and Limits
HR and onboarding questions: hr@novatech.example.
Technical access problems: helpdesk@novatech.example.

These are demonstration addresses.

This guide does not define benefits enrollment deadlines,
equipment delivery guarantees, or probation duration.
Refer those questions to HR or IT as appropriate.
