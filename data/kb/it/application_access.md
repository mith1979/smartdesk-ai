# NovaTech Solutions — Application and Workspace Access

This fictional guide was created for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
Downloaded tickets inspired its topics; they did not provide
the procedures or verified resolutions below.

## Scope
This guide covers requests for access to company-managed
Jira boards, Slack workspaces or channels, and Asana projects.

Installing an application or receiving a license does not
automatically grant access to its projects or workspaces.

## Requesting Access
Send a request to helpdesk@novatech.example with:
- Your NovaTech employee email.
- Application name.
- Exact project, board, workspace, or channel name.
- Business reason.
- Required permission level, if known.
- Reporting manager's written approval.

If you do not know the required permission level,
describe the tasks you need to perform.

## Approval and Provisioning
The reporting manager approves the business need.
The project or workspace owner approves the permission level.
IT provisions access after the required approvals.

Access is limited to the permissions needed for the approved work.
Manager approval alone does not mean access is already enabled.

If a new paid license is needed, IT follows the
Software Installation and License Requests guide.

## Access Still Unavailable
After IT confirms provisioning:
1. Confirm you are signed in with your NovaTech employee account.
2. Sign out and sign in again.
3. Open the project or workspace link supplied by its owner.
4. If access still fails, reply to IT with the exact error
   and the affected project or workspace name.

Do not use another employee's account.

## Changes and Removal
For additional permissions, submit a new request describing
the business need and include manager approval.

Notify IT when access is no longer required.
Project or workspace owners coordinate removal with IT.

## Support and Limits
Contact: helpdesk@novatech.example.
This is a demonstration address.

This guide does not guarantee an approval or completion time.
It does not authorize access to every project in an application.
SmartDesk can collect request details but cannot grant permissions.
