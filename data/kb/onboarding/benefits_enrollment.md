# NovaTech Solutions — Benefits Enrollment

This is a fictional guide generated for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All company rules, contacts, and processes are demonstration values.

## Eligibility and Enrollment Window
Full-time employees, including employees on probation, may submit benefits enrollment choices within 30 calendar days of their employment start date. HR confirms which plans are available to the employee. The enrollment window does not establish insurance coverage dates or a right to a particular plan.

## Submitting Choices
Open NovaTech People Portal > Benefits > Enrollment. Review the available plan information and submit the requested choices through the secure portal. If no enrollment task appears, contact hr@novatech.example with your employee email and start date. Do not send beneficiary details, identity documents, or medical information through SmartDesk.

## Confirmation
Submission is a request for enrollment. Check the portal for HR confirmation and any follow-up task. If HR requests additional information, supply it through the secure channel specified by HR. Do not assume that submitting a form activates coverage immediately.

## Missed Window or Changed Circumstances
If the 30-calendar-day window has passed or your circumstances change, ask HR about the available next steps. Late enrollment and changes are subject to HR review and applicable plan terms. This guide does not promise an exception or retroactive coverage.

## Support and Limits
Contact HR for plan availability, premiums, dependent eligibility, coverage dates, and coverage disputes. Those details are not defined in this demonstration guide. SmartDesk cannot enroll an employee, inspect personal benefits records, or determine whether a specific treatment is covered.
