# NovaTech Solutions — New-Starter Buddy Programme

This is a fictional guide generated for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All company rules, contacts, and processes are demonstration values.

## Purpose and Duration
The buddy programme offers practical orientation during a new full-time employee first 30 calendar days. A buddy helps the employee understand everyday ways of working and find appropriate contacts. The buddy is not a replacement for the reporting manager, HR, or IT.

## Assignment
The reporting manager nominates a buddy and introduces them through company email by the end of the employee first working day. If no introduction arrives, the employee should ask the manager. If the manager is unavailable, contact hr@novatech.example with the employee email and start date.

## Suggested Check-Ins
Arrange a short introduction in the first working week and a weekly check-in during the programme. Discuss team communication channels, meeting routines, office navigation, and where to find reviewed policies. Meeting times should be agreed around both employees work commitments.

## Limits of the Buddy Role
A buddy cannot approve leave, remote work, expenses, equipment purchases, or application permissions. Follow the relevant policy and approval process for those requests. Do not share passwords, MFA recovery codes, payroll records, or confidential personal information with a buddy.

## Changing the Assignment
If a buddy is unavailable or the arrangement is not working, ask the reporting manager to nominate another buddy. HR can help coordinate when needed. This guide does not guarantee an immediate replacement, extend the programme automatically, or define performance-review or probation outcomes.
