# NovaTech Solutions — Email on Company-Managed Phones

This is a fictional guide generated for the SmartDesk AI capstone.
All company rules, contacts, and processes are demonstration values.

## Scope
This guide covers NovaTech email on company-managed phones enrolled by IT. It does not authorize company email on personal phones. Using an authenticator app for MFA is a separate process and does not itself authorize mobile email access.

## Before Setup
Confirm that IT has enrolled the company phone and assigned the approved email application. Request mobile email access through helpdesk@novatech.example with your employee email, phone asset ID, operating system, business reason, and written manager approval. Do not send device unlock codes or passwords.

## Adding the Account
Open the email application installed or approved by IT. Add your NovaTech employee email, complete the company sign-in screen, and finish the required MFA verification. If an unrecognized server or certificate warning appears, stop and contact IT. Do not enter credentials into an unofficial application.

## Email Not Synchronizing
Check whether the phone has an internet connection and whether the application displays a sign-in request. After a password change, sign in again using the current password. If mail still does not synchronize, send IT the error text, last successful synchronization time, and whether web email works. Do not attach mailbox contents.

## Support and Limits
Contact helpdesk@novatech.example for failed enrollment or account access. This guide does not define personal-phone exceptions, remote-wipe procedures, or a guaranteed synchronization interval. SmartDesk cannot enroll a phone, approve access, or bypass MFA.
